Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information IOGames ("we", "us") collects when you visit iogames.example, how we use it, and the choices you have. We built this site so you can play without creating an account, and we try to collect as little as possible.

Information we collect

Information you provide

We do not require registration or an account to browse or play games. If you contact us by email, we receive whatever information you choose to include, such as your email address and message content.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we may automatically collect basic technical data when you visit, including:

  • Pages visited and time spent on the site
  • Browser type, device type, and operating system
  • Approximate location derived from IP address (country/region level)
  • Referring website or search terms that led you here

This data is used in aggregate to understand how the site is used and to fix problems — it is not used to personally identify you.

Cookies & local storage

We may use cookies or browser local storage for basic functionality (such as remembering your last search or sort preference) and for anonymous analytics. You can clear or block cookies at any time through your browser settings; the site will still work, though some preferences may not be remembered.

Third-party games & embedded content

Games on IOGames are loaded from third-party developers and hosting providers. When you play a game, your browser may communicate directly with that provider's servers. Those providers operate under their own privacy policies, which are outside of our control — we encourage you to review them if you have concerns.

Advertising

If IOGames displays advertising, ad partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve relevant ads and measure performance. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your browser or device settings.

Children's privacy

IOGames is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Data retention & security

We retain analytics data only as long as needed for the purposes described above and take reasonable measures to protect it. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the site after changes are posted means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach us via the Contact page.
