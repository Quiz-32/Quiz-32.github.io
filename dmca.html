We respond to clear and complete notices of alleged copyright infringement in accordance with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) and equivalent international laws.

Filing a takedown notice

If you believe that content available on Quiz-32 infringes a copyright you own or control, please send a written notice to dmca@iogames.example that includes the following:

  1. A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or a person authorized to act on their behalf.
  2. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
  3. Identification of the material that is claimed to be infringing, including the specific URL on Quiz-32 where it can be found.
  4. Your contact information, including an email address and, if available, a phone number.
  5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  6. A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on their behalf.

Incomplete notices cannot be processed. Please make sure all six items above are included before submitting your request.

What happens next

Once we receive a valid notice, we will review it and, where appropriate, remove or disable access to the reported content in a timely manner. We may also notify the party that provided the content of the claim.

Counter-notification

If you believe content was removed or disabled as a result of a mistake or misidentification, you may submit a counter-notification containing:

  • Your physical or electronic signature
  • Identification of the material removed and its location before removal
  • A statement, under penalty of perjury, that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed by mistake
  • Your name, address, and phone number, and a statement consenting to the jurisdiction of the appropriate court

Repeat infringers

Quiz-32 maintains a policy of restricting or removing access to content submitted by parties found to be repeat infringers.
